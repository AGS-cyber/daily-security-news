Three distinct CVEs frame the day: a PHP memory-corruption bug CISA has now confirmed as exploited, an Apple CoreGraphics fix reaching older devices, and a patched Android Authenticator flaw that turns a permissionless app into a token thief. Elsewhere, a hospital's ambulance diversion, a botnet backend severed by a national agency, and a university test environment holding data on more than half a million people.

Actively exploited

CISA added the PHP2Shell flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, confirming real-world exploitation CISA Confirms Active Exploitation of Two PHP2Shell Flaws Enabling Remote Code Execution. The detail supplied covers CVE-2026-109618, a memory-corruption issue in PHP's out-of-memory handling: longjmp() error paths can leave request memory uninitialized before an allocation, and later cleanup may read or branch on it, culminating in remote code execution. NVD rates it 9.2 CRITICAL under CVSS 4.0, with a vector of network-reachable, no privileges and no user interaction — but high attack complexity, which is worth noting before treating it as trivially exploitable. Affected ranges end before PHP 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12. The KEV entry carries a federal remediation due date three days after the addition; that is a US federal requirement, not a universal deadline. The excerpt describes two flaws both rated 9.2, but only CVE-2026-109618 is detailed in the supplied intelligence — treat the second as unreviewed here.

Apple backported CVE-2026-86950 to older iPhones and iPads Apple Backports Fix for Exploited CoreGraphics Flaw to Older iPhones and iPads. The out-of-bounds write in CoreGraphics is rated 8.8 HIGH and requires user interaction: processing a maliciously crafted file (CVSS 3.1 AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R). Fixes land in iOS and iPadOS 18.7.11 and 26.7.1, macOS Sequoia 15.8.1 and macOS Tahoe 26.7.1. The KEV record notes Apple is aware of a report that the issue may have been exploited in an extremely sophisticated attack against specific targeted individuals on iOS before iOS 27 — hedged language, and targeted rather than opportunistic. Analysis: the backport's practical value is for older devices that would otherwise sit outside the patch path, but the crafted-file prerequisite means the exposure is to users who can be induced to open something, not to the network at large. That inference rests on the supplied vector, and the underlying report's specifics were not supplied.

Breaches

A hospital diverted ambulances to other health systems in two cities for about two hours and suspended outpatient surgeries and appointments for the remainder of the day following a cyberattack University of New Mexico hospital diverts ambulances after cyberattack. The supplied text does not characterise the incident as ransomware or name a cause; what it does document is operational and patient-care impact, which is the part that matters when assessing third-party and clinical risk.

The University of Copenhagen confirmed attackers accessed a test environment containing personal data belonging to 525,000 people University of Copenhagen data breach exposes data of 525,000 people. Analysis: test environments repeatedly surface in breach disclosures because they often hold copies of production data under lighter controls — but no specifics on how the data got there or how access was obtained were supplied, so the mechanism and any control failure remain unconfirmed.

Vulnerabilities

Microsoft fixed CVE-2026-80097 in Authenticator for Android, an improper-authentication flaw (CWE-287) rated 8.6 HIGH that let a malicious app with no permissions pose as a trusted Microsoft app such as Teams and collect sign-in tokens for a victim's work account Microsoft Authenticator Flaw Let Permissionless Android Apps Steal Work Account Tokens. The fix is in Authenticator for Android 6.2606.4246. The CVSS vector is local (AV:L) with user interaction required, and scope changed; the KEV lookup is a confirmed catalog miss, which says nothing about whether exploitation has occurred. For enterprise fleets, the significance is the target: work-account tokens are the currency of session hijacking, and the vector is a user installing something on a managed Android device.

Tailscale is working through almost a dozen SSH vulnerabilities that could be exploited to steal credentials, crash servers and gain root privileges Tailscale warns of SSH flaws allowing root privilege escalation. Patch status is described as still in flight; no CVE IDs, affected versions or exploitation evidence were supplied, so the practical triage question — which of the dozen matter and which deployments are exposed — cannot be answered from this evidence alone.

Research

Lazarus has been attributed to an espionage campaign tracked as Operation FlashHole against at least three South Korean organisations in construction, software, and wire-communication equipment manufacturing, active since at least July 2025 according to Kaspersky Lazarus Abuses South Korean Security Software to Deploy Malware Using Post-Quantum Encryption. Two tradecraft details stand out: abuse of legitimate South Korean security software, and post-quantum encryption used to protect payloads. Analysis: the first borrows trust from software that local defenders are inclined to allow; the second suggests the operators are optimising against retrospective decryption of captured traffic rather than against live detection. Neither claim about intent is supplied — it is an inference from the tooling described.

Industry

Australia's signals directorate gained access to an Amadey operator's backend and cut it off from roughly 180,000 infected computers worldwide, including machines in Australia Australia Gets Into Amadey Malware Backend, Cuts Off Access to 180,000 Infected Computers. The agency says the criminal can no longer access or steal data from those systems. Analysis: this is disruption rather than prosecution, and it raises the usual unanswered question for defenders — whether cutting the controller leaves the implants dormant, orphaned, or ready for a new operator. The supplied text does not say whether remediation or cleanup of the 180,000 hosts accompanied the takedown.
