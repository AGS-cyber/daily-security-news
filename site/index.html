Exploitation of two critical PHP flaws leads the briefing, with industrial vendors confirming exposure to the same issue. A Brotli decoder flaw forces cross-ecosystem patching, while token theft and exposed keys dominate the breach reporting.

Actively exploited

Two critical PHP remote-code-execution flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 and tracked as PHP2Shell, have been successfully exploited in the wild, according to PHP2Shell security flaws now sucessfully exploited in the wild. Both NVD entries carry a CVSS 4.0 score of 9.2 Critical, with network attack vector, high attack complexity, no required privileges and no user interaction. NVD lists vulnerable PHP ranges ending before 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12. The story does not supply exploit mechanics, targeted victims or the initial access path. The excerpt does not give victim sectors, the relay's delivery method or the MFA configuration of affected tenants, so the excerpt does not establish an MFA bypass. Analysis: replaying a post-authentication session is potent because it reuses trust already granted, but the supplied evidence stops short of explaining how the relay was positioned.
