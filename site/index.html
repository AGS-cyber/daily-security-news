Patchstack reports active in-the-wild exploitation of a maximum-severity flaw in the Bricksforge plugin for WordPress Attackers Exploit CVSS 10.0 Bricksforge WordPress Flaw Enabling Unauthenticated RCE. CVE-2026-85097 is an unauthenticated arbitrary file upload, rated CWE-434, in versions up to and including 3.1.8.9. The NVD record describes the chain precisely: the attacker first obtains a valid nonce through the bricksforge_regenerate_nonce AJAX endpoint, uploads a GIF/PHP polyglot to the temporary upload directory where MIME validation is performed correctly, then submits a form whose crafted temporaryFileUploads parameter points the server-side path at the already-validated GIF while the attacker-controlled URL field ends in .php — yielding arbitrary PHP execution. Note the score discrepancy: the story reports CVSS 10.0, while the supplied NVD record gives 9.8 (CRITICAL, AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N). The gap is immaterial to exposure but worth stating, since the 10.0 appears in the headline framing.

SonicWall's SMA 1000 is the second front Critical SonicWall SMA 1000 Flaw Targeted in 135 Exploitation Attempts After Patch. CVE-2026-102255 carries a supplied CVSS of 10.0 and describes a pre-authentication SSRF in the SMA1000 Appliance Work Place interface, arising from an unintended alternate access path: a remote unauthenticated attacker can direct the appliance to issue requests on their behalf, reach internal functionality and perform unauthorized operations (CWE-441, CWE-918). The story's headline reports 135 exploitation attempts after the patch, and the excerpt states exploitation was observed within days of public disclosure. No affected version list was supplied, and KEV shows a confirmed miss. Analysis: for internet-facing SMA 1000 appliances, the combination of pre-auth reachability and a short disclosure-to-exploitation window is the operative fact; the absence from KEV changes nothing for defenders who can see their own logs.
