Actively exploited

Microsoft’s latest Patch Tuesday addresses a record-breaking 1,235 flaws and includes one actively exploited Outlook zero-day, making this the lead patching event for Outlook-heavy estates Microsoft November 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes 1,235 flaws, Outlook zero-day. No CVE or affected-version detail for the Outlook bug is supplied here.

Attackers are already exploiting a critical authentication bypass in Xen Orchestra to gain administrator privileges and take over XCP-ng virtualization hosts Critical Xen Orchestra auth bypass flaw exploited to hijack VM servers. No CVE or patch detail is supplied for that flaw.

CISA added four flaws to its KEV catalog citing evidence of active exploitation, including CVE-2026-123218 in IBM Aspera Faspex CISA Warns of Active Exploitation of OpenSSH, GPUStack, FreeSWITCH, and IBM Flaws. The enriched Aspera bug is Critical at 9.8, an incorrect-authorization issue (CWE-863) affecting 5.0.0 through before 5.0.19.1, and it carries a CISA remediation requirement with a near-term federal due date. The other three entries — GPUStack, SignalWire FreeSWITCH and OpenBSD OpenSSH — were not individually enriched here, so their severity and deadlines should not be inferred from the Aspera record.

Google’s Chrome update fixes nine vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-125350, a high-severity use-after-free in XML that is under active exploitation and is described as the eighth actively exploited Chrome zero-day of the year Google Issues Chrome Security Update for Eighth Actively Exploited Zero-Day of 2026. NVD ties it to Chrome before 157.0.8103.50 and crafted HTML pages that can execute code inside the sandbox; no CVSS score is supplied, and KEV lists it with a federal remediation requirement.

Vulnerabilities

N-able has taken its hosted N-central environment offline and is telling on-premises customers to shut down servers until further notice after public disclosure of more than 30 vulnerabilities tracked as Able2Shell N-able Takes N-central Cloud Offline After Researcher Discloses 33 Zero-Days with PoC Exploits. The disclosure is described as 33 zero-days with PoC exploits, while the excerpt says more than 30 vulnerabilities and supplies no CVE IDs or root cause. Analysis: the shutdown advice turns this from routine patching into an operational stop for affected N-central users.

Breaches

Russian state-linked hackers compromised government email servers in Romania and Kazakhstan to intercept messages exchanged with NATO and European Union officials, according to researchers Russian hackers compromised government email servers to steal NATO, EU correspondence. The reported reach beyond the first victim is the espionage signal; no CVE, malware family or affected product is supplied.

More than a dozen organizations were hit in a Qubole supply-chain attack in which compromised IAM credentials were used to steal customer files, secrets, keys, environment variables and credentials Over a Dozen Organizations Hit in Qubole Supply Chain Attack. The credential theft is the stated vector; the excerpt does not name victims, give a more precise count or describe remediation.

Industry

The French government attributed an attack on refrigeration controllers at about 700 retail sites to an IRGC cyber unit, saying intruders changed defrost settings to high temperatures, causing food losses and at least 15 fires France Blames Iran for Sabotage Attack on Refrigeration Controllers at About 700 Retail Sites. The public attribution is the notable element: a government formally naming a state cyber unit for OT sabotage against civilian retail infrastructure. Analysis: the excerpt does not describe initial access or whether the technique was novel, so the significance rests on the formal naming rather than on supplied technical detail.
