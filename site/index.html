Lead with PAN-OS Palo Alto Networks warns of PAN-OS RCE flaw exploited in attacks. CVE-2026-0322 is a code-injection flaw (CWE-94) rated 8.6 High under CVSS 4.0, and it has been exploited against firewalls. NVD places it in the management interface and says it lets an authenticated attacker with network access execute arbitrary code as root — so this is not an anonymous internet-wide hit: exploitation depends on both credentials and reachability of the management plane. Affected configurations include PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls and Panorama; Cloud NGFW and Prisma Access are listed as not impacted. CISA added it to KEV with a required action built around vendor mitigations and per-asset exposure evaluation, and a federal remediation deadline only days later. Analysis: the operative question is whether a management interface is reachable beyond trusted internal addresses — the same exposure the vendor's own guidance tells operators to close. That deadline binds federal agencies under the cited directive, not everyone.
