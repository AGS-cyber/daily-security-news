The highest-severity bugs of the week aren't hard to reach — they don't require credentials at all. A pair of maximum-scoring flaws in a widely deployed email gateway and in self-managed GitLab are both confirmed exploited and both in CISA's catalog, while ransomware crews joined a virtualization campaign and a Chinese APT chained browser and OS zero-days against civil society.

Actively exploited

Start with Cisco Secure Email Gateway Flaw Exploited in the Wild, Enables Root Command Execution. CVE-2026-76461 is a 9.8 in Cisco AsyncOS for Secure Email Gateway, and the mechanics are what should worry you: it's a CWE-89 injection reachable through insufficient validation in the email parsing logic. An unauthenticated, remote attacker sends a crafted message containing malicious SQL, and the result is arbitrary command execution as root on the underlying operating system. No account, no interaction, no foothold required — the gateway that exists to sanitize inbound mail is itself the entry point. Affected builds include AsyncOS before 15.5.5-014, 16.0 before 16.0.4-302, and 16.5 before 16.5.0-780. It's in KEV, and the catalog entry carries a three-day remediation window for federal agencies — an unusually tight one, and a reasonable proxy for how urgently Cisco's own PSIRT treats it. If you run SEG at the perimeter, this is the top of today's list; the appliance's position in the mail path means compromise gives an attacker both root and a privileged vantage point inside the network.

Maximum Severity GitLab Flaw Puts Supply Chains at Risk is the same story told about your source code. CVE-2026-85706 in GitLab CE/EE carries a CVSS of 10.0 and is also KEV-listed with the same three-day federal window. It's a CWE-22 path traversal: improper path confinement plus missing authentication enforcement in the repository commits API lets an unauthenticated user read arbitrary files from the server. The CVSS vector is worth reading closely — scope changed, confidentiality and integrity both high, availability unaffected. Quiet exfiltration, in other words, aimed at exactly the assets that hurt most: CI/CD variables, deploy keys, tokens, and the source itself. Affected ranges run from 18.7 up to before 19.1.8, 19.2 before 19.2.6, and 19.3 before 19.3.2. Anyone exposing a self-managed instance to the internet should assume secrets in reach of that API are burned.

Ransomware crews have now joined attacks exploiting a critical VMware vCenter RCE, per CISA: Critical VMware RCE flaw now exploited by ransomware gangs. CISA's warning matters less for novelty than for the shift in operator profile: this flaw has had a patch available for a while, and the arrival of extortion groups means the window for quiet patching is closing. Virtualization management planes are high-value, low-redundancy targets — encrypt or corrupt vCenter and you've taken the estate with it.

China-Linked Hackers Exploit Chrome-Windows Zero-Day Chain to Deploy GRIMWEDGE describes a different kind of tradecraft. A China-linked cluster tracked as UTA0560 used spear-phishing against multiple NGOs, chaining recently patched Chrome and Windows flaws to deliver GRIMWEDGE, a JavaScript backdoor. The lesson is the chain, not either bug: a browser renderer flaw plus an OS privilege-escalation primitive turns a single click into a durable espionage implant, and NGOs remain a soft target with hard intelligence value.

Research

'Sandworm' Chains Cisco Vulnerabilities to Deploy Cyclops Blink is the one to watch rather than act on today. Sandworm is again spreading Cyclops Blink, an upgraded build of the botnet malware the FBI disrupted in 2022, and is doing so by chaining Cisco vulnerabilities. A rebuilt botnet built on router and edge-device implants is the harder problem of the two: those devices are rarely inventoried, rarely patched, and sit outside most endpoint telemetry. Treat this as a signal that the group is re-establishing long-haul access infrastructure, not as a single incident to close out.

Breaches

Two confirmed breaches round out the picture, and both are about third parties and utilities rather than exotic exploits. CenterPoint Energy confirmed a breach after a hacker leaked 7.5 million customer records, Texas Utility CenterPoint Energy Confirms Breach After Hacker Leaks Data — a Texas utility, extortion-driven, and a reminder that critical-infrastructure operators now carry the same data-liability profile as retailers. Separately, Japan's Digital Agency says roughly 240,000 people had personal information stolen after attackers exploited a vulnerability in a VPN product, 240,000 Hit by Data Breach at Japan’s Digital Agency. That's the second story this week where the security control — email gateway, VPN concentrator — was the way in. Inventory the appliances you trust implicitly and check them against known-exploited lists before you do anything else.

Vulnerabilities

Finally, Apple Patches 200 Vulnerabilities With New iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate 27 Releases: Apple shipped iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate 27 with fixes for around 200 vulnerabilities, including kernel flaws enabling memory corruption, privilege escalation, system termination, and information leaks. No known exploitation, so this is routine hygiene rather than an emergency — but kernel privilege-escalation bugs are the second half of the chains described above, and update latency on Apple hardware in your fleet is the variable that decides whether the next browser zero-day stays a sandbox escape or becomes a full compromise.
