Today's editorial list is dominated by confirmed exploitation: critical, unauthenticated flaws in Cisco's SD-WAN management plane, Citrix NetScaler edge appliances, Apple's client platforms, and internet-facing Zimbra mail servers. All four carry CISA KEV entries and vendor-confirmed or reported in-the-wild use. Below that, two breaches — one a nine-figure crypto theft blamed on a third-party security product — and two research items on espionage phishing and malware delivery through a trusted AI platform.

Actively exploited

Cisco's Catalyst SD-WAN Manager carries the day's highest score: CVE-2026-76504 is a 9.8 CVSS 3.1 flaw in API session authentication management, caused by improper handling of URI encoding in an HTTP request, letting an unauthenticated remote attacker bypass an authentication rule and reach the API as the admin user (CWE-177) Cisco warns of new SD-WAN zero-day exploited in attacks. CISA has confirmed active exploitation; its required action is to apply mitigations per vendor instructions. Network teams should treat the management plane itself as the incident, not just the edge.

Citrix NetScaler is next, with two exploited flaws in the same KEV batch Government, Finance Orgs Targeted in Weeks-Long NetScaler Zero-Day Attacks. CVE-2026-88771 is an improper input validation bug (CWE-20) in NetScaler ADC and Gateway, rated 9.5 under CVSS 4.0 and described as allowing an unauthenticated attacker to execute arbitrary commands. CVE-2026-88772, also 9.5, is a memory-buffer flaw (CWE-119) leading to remote code execution or denial of service. Both affect ADC and Gateway before 14.1-73.37 and 13.1-64.23, including FIPS and NDcPP builds. The reported campaigns ran for weeks against government and finance victims — long dwell time on an appliance that terminates trust for everything behind it.

Apple's CVE-2026-86950 is a lower-scoring but tightly targeted out-of-bounds write (CWE-787, 8.8 CVSS) Apple Zero-Day Vulnerability Weaponized in Targeted Attacks. Processing a maliciously crafted file can yield arbitrary code execution; Apple says it is aware of a report that the issue may have been exploited in an extremely sophisticated attack against specific targeted individuals on iOS before iOS 27. Fixes are in iOS and iPadOS 26.7.1 and in macOS Sequoia 15.8.1 and macOS Tahoe 26.7.1. Merchants of spyware, not mass criminals, is the safe read — but the affected populations are the ones who cannot wait for a patch cycle.

Zimbra rounds out the section: CVE-2026-73570 is an unauthenticated OS command injection (CWE-78, 8.9) in ZCS before 10.1.20, exploitable when the optional zimbra-snmp package is installed and SNMP notifications are enabled, via crafted SMTP requests that can execute commands as the Zimbra user Unauthenticated command injection on internet-facing mail servers: tracking CVE-2026-73570. Microsoft's write-up covers observed attack paths, detection opportunities and mitigation guidance — useful because the precondition means not every deployment is exposed.

Analysis. The common thread is pre-authentication access to systems that broker trust. KEV inclusion across all four confirms exploitation is real, not theoretical; two of the entries list ransomware campaign use as unknown, which is an absence of data rather than an absence of risk.

Breaches

Bitget says attackers who took $387.5 million breached its systems by exploiting a zero-day in third-party security products Bitget hacked via zero-day in third-party security products. The figure and the vector are the story; the unstated part is which product, which matters enormously to anyone else running it.

South Africa is seeking outside help after an attack on air traffic control, with a ransomware toolkit found installed on at least one operational network South Africa Seeks Help After Cyberattack Targets Air Traffic Control. No CVE data accompanies either story.

Research

Microsoft attributes a phishing campaign using fake event invitations to Russia's Star Blizzard, targeting people and organizations tied to Ukraine and affecting more than 100 organizations since January, mostly in the U.S. and U.K. Russia's Star Blizzard Targets 100+ Organizations With Fake Event Invites to Deliver Backdoor. At least one computer was infected and a Windows backdoor was delivered. The reported breach count is not established in what we have.

ChatGPT Custom GPTs are being abused to pose as legitimate product offerings and funnel victims to malicious sites that use ClickFix lures to deliver a remote access trojan, observed by Huntress Attackers Abuse ChatGPT Custom GPTs to Deliver RAT via ClickFix Lures. The angle worth taking seriously: the lure now inherits the reputation of the AI platform hosting it, so user education built on "is this a known brand" no longer holds.
