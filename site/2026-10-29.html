CISA's catalog addition for a VMware ESX logging flaw confirms exploitation in a legal-sector espionage case, while Cisco and Palo Alto Networks both report live attacks on edge appliances. Also in today's briefing: a critical Linux kernel TCP bug with an AI-written exploit, a reported federal identity breach, and a large Chrome extension hijack.

Actively exploited

CISA added CVE-2026-41709 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after the VMware ESX flaw was tied to attacks on the U.S. legal sector by the China-nexus actor Warp Panda China-Linked Hackers Exploited VMware ESX Zero-Day to Reduce Audit Trail Visibility. On paper it is small: NVD describes insufficient logging (CWE-778) that lets a malicious administrator perform operations without them being recorded, scoring 2.7 LOW with high privileges required and no user interaction. KEV membership, not the score, is the signal. CISA's required action — apply vendor mitigations under its risk-based federal patching directive — binds federal agencies, not everyone, and the supplied text does not describe the post-exploitation chain.

Cisco released patches for CVE-2026-76418, an 8.6 HIGH flaw in the Remote Access SSL VPN service of Secure Firewall ASA and FTD software, saying it has been exploited in the wild Cisco Patches Actively Exploited ASA and FTD VPN Flaw That Can Crash Firewalls. An unauthenticated remote attacker can send a crafted request that reloads the device, a denial of service, but the trigger requires secondary authentication configured on a tunnel group, so exposure varies by deployment. It is not in KEV; a catalog miss is not proof of safety.

Palo Alto Networks says active exploitation has been observed against management interfaces exposed to the internet, targeting the recently patched PHP2Shell flaws Attackers Exploit PHP2Shell Flaws in Internet-Exposed Palo Alto Networks Management Interfaces. No CVE identifiers came with this item, so the vendor advisory is the authoritative list. Analysis: an administrative plane reachable from the internet is the exposure itself; patching narrows it, and the supplied text says nothing about what attackers did afterwards.

Vulnerabilities

CVE-2026-98432, a use-after-free in the Linux kernel's TCP handling, carries a 9.8 CRITICAL rating and affects Ubuntu, RHEL and other distributions Linux Kernel TCP Flaw With 9.8 CVSS Score Affects Ubuntu, Red Hat and Other Distributions. The exploit is the notable part: Anthropic said its AI model wrote one that lets a remote attacker with no credentials run commands as root. The CVE is not in KEV, and the structured record supplies no affected version ranges, so patch status must be checked per distribution rather than inferred. Analysis: the vector — network, no privileges, no user interaction, full confidentiality, integrity and availability impact — is about as bad as kernel bugs get.

Breaches

More than 41,000 DHS employee records were reportedly leaked after personal and work information was allegedly stolen from an Okta single sign-on system earlier this year Over 41,000 DHS Employee Records Leaked Online. Both the leak and the theft are described as alleged; no confirmation, additional scope or remediation was supplied. Identity providers stay the centre of federal breach risk for the obvious reason: a compromised SSO tenant sits upstream of everything it authenticates.

Research

319 Chrome extensions with a combined 13.4 million reported installs were hijacking searches, redirecting queries through tracking servers to generate advertising revenue from Bing and Yahoo Chrome extensions with 13.4 million installs caught hijacking searches. No CVE or extension list was supplied. Analysis: install counts describe the internet, not your estate — the operative question is which of these sit in your browser fleet.

Cisco Talos documented NIK C2, a new AI-integrated command-and-control framework observed in the wild NIK C2: The new AI-enabled command-and-control framework. The excerpt offers no victimology, tooling detail or attribution. Analysis: "AI-integrated" is a descriptor, not a capability claim; the practical signal is model assistance folded into unglamorous C2 work rather than anything autonomous.
