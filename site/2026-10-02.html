Actively exploited

Fortinet's FortiMail path traversal bug takes the lead: CVE-2026-104286 is a CVSS 9.8 Critical, CWE-22 flaw that vendor data says lets an unauthenticated attacker write arbitrary files via crafted HTTP or HTTPS requests, covering FortiMail 7.2.0 through 7.4.8, 7.6.0 through 7.6.6 and 8.0.0 through 8.0.1 Fortinet warns of critical FortiMail flaw exploited in zero-day attacks. CISA's KEV catalog marks it known exploited, and the vendor's warning describes zero-day attacks used to execute unauthorized code or commands. KEV's required action directs federal agencies to vendor mitigations under BOD 26-04 and assigns a remediation due date; that is a US federal deadline, not a universal one. The combination — exploited in the wild, unauthenticated, internet-facing mail gateways, critical severity — is why this ranks first.
