Four distinct CVEs run through today's selection, and three of them are confirmed in-the-wild targets now sitting on CISA's catalog [[s20]]. A VMware vCenter authentication bypass led the day as a zero-day attributed to a suspected espionage group [[s28]], while a Cisco Expressway flaw moved from public proof-of-concept to a real intrusion [[s27]]. Alongside them: an unusual "agentic" breach claim from a telco, an AI-assisted disruption at the European Commission, a multi-tenant cloud isolation bug in KVM, and an AI pentesting tool in a university campaign.

Actively exploited

The lead is CVE-2026-123890, a 9.8-rated authentication bypass in VMware's Directory Service (CWE-288) that the suspected China-nexus group Armory Castle, formerly UNC5221, is attributed with exploiting as a zero-day to gain administrative access and steal data China-Linked Hackers Exploit Critical VMware vCenter Zero-Day to Gain Admin Access and Steal Data. The CVSS vector is network-reachable with no privileges or user interaction required. CISA lists it as known exploited and directs agencies to apply mitigations per vendor instructions under its risk-based patching directive, with a federal remediation due date attached — a deadline for federal agencies, not a universal one. NVD's fixed builds are 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200, 8.0 U3m and 5.2.5, with Telco Cloud Platform and Telco Cloud Infrastructure also listed as affected. KEV records ransomware campaign use as unknown. The attribution is supplied as suspected, not proven.

The catalog announcement names four additions; the supplied excerpt shows three of them CISA Adds Four Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to Catalog. The spread matters more than the count: alongside the two 9.8 vCenter and Expressway entries sits SAP NetWeaver CVE-2025-42884 at just 6.5, Medium, a CWE-943 data-query flaw allowing an unauthenticated attacker to inject JNDI environment properties or pass a URL used in JNDI lookups, reaching an unintended provider and potentially disclosing or modifying server information with no availability impact. Analysis: severity score is a poor proxy for urgency here — KEV membership and confirmed exploitation are the signal, and a Medium scoring entry can belong on the same remediation list as a Critical one.

CVE-2026-76466 is the case study for why. Threat actors exploited the Cisco Expressway Series flaw against a Russian organization shortly after a public PoC appeared, breaching four devices and reaching the internal network Hackers Exploit Critical Cisco Expressway Flaw, Breach Four Devices and Reach Internal Network. The impact is root: an unauthenticated remote attacker with network access can execute arbitrary operating-system commands on the underlying device, per NVD a consequence of persistent cookies plus insufficient client certificate validation when TLS Verify Mode is enabled for clustering. Edge devices that terminate untrusted connectivity and hand back root are the shortest path inward, which is why the PoC-to-breach gap is the number to watch.

Breaches

Bell Canada says an "agentic" threat actor breached its systems and stole enterprise customer data, including from a system used to order and provision telecommunications circuits Bell Canada says 'agentic' hacker stole enterprise customer data. That characterization is the company's own; the excerpt supplies no detail on tooling, autonomy, or how much of the intrusion was machine-driven. Analysis: treat "agentic" as a claim to be tested, not a technique — but if it holds, it is an early concrete data point on autonomous intrusion at a large telco.

The European Commission confirmed an AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted its services, taking some offline to contain the incident while stating it has no evidence of a data breach European Commission Confirms AI-Assisted Cyberattack Disrupted Services. Two AI-framed incidents in one cycle, neither with supplied technical detail, is a pattern worth noting rather than a conclusion.

Separately, J.P. Morgan Chase warned of a data breach after an attack at a third-party service provider exposed customers' personal information and marketing data Vendor data breach exposes Chase, Visa, Mastercard customer info. The headline points to Visa and Mastercard customer data too, though the excerpt details only Chase's notification and names no attacker. Supplier-side exposure remains the financial sector's structural weakness.

Vulnerabilities

CVE-2026-99312 is a good reminder that hypervisor bugs are cloud-provider bugs. Researchers used a flaw in Linux KVM to read roughly 127 GB of a cloud server's memory from inside a virtual machine of their own, memory that on a shared host can include other customers' VMs Linux KVM Flaw CVE-2026-99312 Lets Guest VMs Access Host Memory on AMD Hosts. It affects only hosts with AMD processors and nested virtualization enabled; NVD rates it 8.8 High with a local vector requiring low privileges. It is not in KEV per the catalog check — which proves nothing about exploitation either way — and fixed kernels were shipping.

Research

A China-linked actor used the open-source Cairn AI penetration-testing tool to persistently attack two universities, and in one case possibly discovered a zero-day, according to researchers Amazon says Chinese hackers used Cairn AI tool to discover apparent zero-day during attack on university. The zero-day is described as apparent and unconfirmed; no CVE is attached in the supplied evidence. Read next to the Bell and Commission claims, it suggests AI assistance is appearing in incident narratives faster than it is being documented in technical detail.
