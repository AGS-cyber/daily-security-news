CISA has added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its known-exploited catalog after they were chained in zero-day attacks, while more than a thousand internet-exposed GitLab servers remain unpatched against an actively exploited maximum-severity RCE. Also in the file: a 1.3 million-device Android TV botnet, a $6 billion crypto heist attributed to North Korea, Apple's 99-country spyware warnings, an SSN-exposing student-aid breach, remotely sabotaged supermarket freezers, and critical Langflow flaws with no KEV listing.

Actively exploited

Both of the day's CVE IDs sit in one story: Ivanti Connect Secure flaws chained in zero-day attacks and now in CISA's catalog Ivanti Patches Two More Zero-Days Exploited in Connect Secure Attack Chain. CVE-2026-111357 is catalogued as a missing-authentication weakness for a critical function and scored 7.0 HIGH (CVSS 3.1, MITRE); NVD's description says the broker's handling of DSCache configuration data lets an attacker escalate to root, and states it has been exploited in the wild. CVE-2026-118527, a predictable-numbers-or-identifiers flaw, has a KEV entry but no NVD record in the supplied evidence, so its severity and affected versions are unknown here. Configuration data for the first flaw covers Connect Secure 25.1.0.0 up to but excluding 25.1.1.3, plus the 22.8 R2 line through R2.5. One inconsistency is worth flagging rather than resolving by convenience: the KEV entry names missing authentication, while the NVD vector is local, high-complexity and low-privilege. CISA's required action is to apply vendor mitigations, follow its risk-based patching guidance, or discontinue the product; the two carry separate federal deadlines, and ransomware use is listed as unknown for both. Analysis: the chaining is the story — an escalation primitive paired with a predictable-identifier flaw is what turns a single appliance bug into an intrusion path — though nothing supplied attributes this to a named actor.

Breaches

The Florida Department of Education says Social Security numbers and other personal data may have been exposed in a breach of its financial aid website, putting data on 4.9 million people at risk Florida Student Aid Website Hack Puts Data of 4.9 Million People at Risk. Separately, a cyberattack on refrigeration systems disrupted stores run by multiple supermarket chains across several European countries after attackers remotely changed temperature settings on freezers and cooling systems Hackers disrupt refrigeration systems at European supermarkets — Analysis: the supplied facts describe immediate operational disruption rather than data loss, which is the notable part.

Vulnerabilities

Two critical Langflow flaws could let a remote attacker with no account bypass authentication or run arbitrary code, according to IBM Critical Langflow Flaws Could Let Unauthenticated Attackers Run Code on Servers. Every release from 1.0.0 through 1.12.8 is affected and 1.13.0 is the only fix. Neither flaw is listed in CISA's KEV catalog, and the excerpt reports no in-the-wild exploitation — which is a catalog miss, not proof that none has occurred.
