Actively exploited

CVE-2026-114713, a high-severity UnrealIRCd flaw rated CVSS 7.7, is in CISA KEV as known exploited Recent UnrealIRCd Vulnerability Exploited in Attacks. NVD says the labeled-response module in 6.0.0 before 6.2.7.1 fails to escape the IRCv3 "label" tag when echoing replies, letting any user on a network with two or more linked servers inject server-to-server commands executed with server authority. Its CVSS vector indicates network access, low privileges and no user interaction, with changed scope. CISA's required action cites vendor mitigations and BOD 26-04; its due date is federal, not universal. Analysis: the linked-server condition limits exposure but concentrates risk in federated IRC estates.
