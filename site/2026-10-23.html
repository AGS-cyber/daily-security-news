The day's most consequential items sit at the management layer rather than the endpoint: a third exploited Arista VCO zero-day in four months, a large-scale credential harvest against FortiGate and GlobalProtect edge devices, a poisoned LiteLLM release, and a vendor-attributed ConnectWise supply-chain intrusion across more than 2,200 tenants. Actively exploited

The lead story is the third actively exploited Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator zero-day in four months Attackers Exploit Third Arista VCO Zero-Day in Four Months. The flaw is a critical-severity SQL injection that lets low-privilege users reach privileged internal functionality and obtain operator privileges. No CVE ID or KEV enrichment was supplied with this story, so it cannot be correlated against the catalog here. Analysis: operator-level access to an SD-WAN orchestrator is a control-plane foothold that reaches every managed edge site behind it; that assessment rests on the described privilege escalation, not on any supplied detail about which victims were touched.

CISA added six vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog CISA Adds Six Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to Catalog. The supplied records detail three of them, all Plex Media Server issues affecting releases before 1.43.3.10861: CVE-2026-96652 and CVE-2026-96655 are server-side request forgery flaws scored 5.3, with the supplied vectors indicating a low-privileged actor and no user interaction, while CVE-2026-96654 — improper neutralization of encoded URI schemes in the searchOne path — is scored 6.9 with no privileges or interaction required. All three are Medium severity; KEV listing rests on evidence of active exploitation. CISA's remediation requirement runs to federal civilian agencies under BOD 26-04, with near-term due dates set for these entries — that is not a universal deadline for other operators. Ransomware campaign use is listed as unknown for all three. The remaining three additions were not detailed in the supplied evidence.

Vulnerabilities

Attackers have been observed attempting to exploit the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities at volume, including operators trying to enrol systems into a Mirai variant called Potassium, but the attempts have been unsuccessful, and reporting suggests weaponization is harder than first assumed PHP2Shell Exploit Attempts Hit Millions as Attackers Try to Deploy Mirai Malware. The two flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, are each scored 9.2 Critical under CVSS 4.0, and can yield unauthenticated remote code execution — the supplied vectors show network reach and no privilege or user-interaction requirement, but rate attack complexity as high, which is consistent with the reported difficulty. Affected PHP ranges end-exclusive at 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12. Neither CVE is in KEV, which is a confirmed catalog miss but not proof that exploitation has not occurred. Analysis: continued mass probing against a high-complexity bug is a reasonable signal that a working exploit will eventually circulate; that is inference from attempt volume, not a supplied finding.
