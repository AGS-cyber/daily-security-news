Virtualization and the network edge dominate: a vCenter authentication bypass was exploited before a patch, PHP flaws reached firewall management interfaces, and CISA added four exploited vulnerabilities. Breach disclosures and an encryption notice round out the day.

Actively exploited

A critical VMware vCenter authentication bypass was exploited as a zero-day by a China-linked espionage group before Broadcom released a patch Newly Patched VMware vCenter Flaw Exploited as Zero-Day by Chinese APT. Analysis: the significance is placement. vCenter is the control plane for virtualization estates, so an authentication bypass there can expose management rather than a single workload. The supplied excerpt does not name the CVE, affected vCenter versions, or the exploitation window; those details are not available here.

Attackers exploited two PHP flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, against PAN-OS management interfaces exposed to the internet, with a firewall among the early PHP2Shell targets Everything Is a Web Application, Including Your Firewall. Both CVEs are in CISA KEV, both are rated CVSS 4.0 9.2 Critical, and both can lead to remote code execution in PHP. Their vectors show network access, no privileges and no user interaction, but high attack complexity. NVD also lists PAN-OS 10.2.0 before 10.2.7 in the affected configurations, so the supplied exposure set extends beyond PHP itself. CISA’s required action is to apply mitigations per vendor instructions.

CISA added four vulnerabilities to KEV on evidence of active exploitation: CVE-2024-10441 in Synology DSM (9.8 Critical), CVE-2026-40217 in LiteLLM (8.8 High), CVE-2026-90436 in Apache Axis (9.8 Critical) and CVE-2026-90817 in REDCap (9.8 Critical) CISA Adds Four Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to Catalog. The set is not uniformly critical: LiteLLM’s vector requires low privileges; Axis is unauthenticated; REDCap is unauthenticated but requires knowledge of a valid public survey hash. Axis 1.x is end-of-life through 1.4, and the supplied description says migrating or using a patched build are the options. Analysis: the spread — NAS management, LiteLLM, a legacy SOAP engine and research data capture — illustrates how KEV risk can sit in internet-reachable middleware rather than headline platforms.

Cisco Expressway exploitation came soon after a public proof of concept: attackers compromised clustered Expressway devices at a Russian organization and prepared tooling for lateral movement Hackers Exploit Cisco Expressway Vulnerability Soon After PoC Release. The excerpt does not name the CVE, the affected versions, or confirm internet exposure. Analysis: the case fits the pattern of edge appliances moving from PoC to intrusion quickly, though a single incident cannot establish a general tempo.
