Three of the six entries CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog sit in enterprise communications and email infrastructure, and one carries a federal remediation deadline only three days out. The same day brought a 1,259-flaw Microsoft patch release, an actively exploited authentication bypass in a virtualization console, and confirmation of a 37.6-million-record retail breach. Four distinct CVE IDs are in scope below — a count of primary records in this selection, not a daily vulnerability total.

Actively exploited

CISA's latest KEV update adds six vulnerabilities on evidence of active exploitation; the three detailed in the supplied material are Cisco BroadWorks, Barracuda Networks appliances and Openwave Webtop Media Transcoder CISA Adds Six Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to Catalog. The Cisco bug, CVE-2026-20320, is an XML external entity flaw in the BroadWorks Open Client Interface parser rated 7.5 High: an unauthenticated, remote attacker sending a crafted XML message to the OCI-P service could read sensitive configuration files with the privileges of the BroadWorks user, with no user interaction and no privileges required. CVE-2026-111560 in Barracuda products is an OS command injection rated 8.6 High under CVSS 4.0, but it is not a zero-day free-for-all — it requires an authenticated administrator to plant a crafted timezone value that later executes with root privileges. CISA's federal due date for that one is the tightest of the set; the Cisco and Openwave deadlines fall later in the month. But those dates bind federal agencies under the cited binding directive, not every organization. CVE-2026-111793, the Openwave flaw, is the outlier on severity: 4.3 Medium, an authenticated user bypassing the webmail experience to convert file types the administrator blocked, affecting versions before 1.1.22.0. KEV membership, not CVSS, is why all three belong on a triage list today.

Microsoft's November release covers 1,259 vulnerabilities, the second consecutive month above a thousand, spread across Windows, Office, SQL Server, SharePoint and developer tooling, plus 165 CVE identifiers issued by other parties Microsoft Patches 1,259 Flaws, Including Exploited Outlook Bug That May Expose NTLMv2 Hashes. The supplied detail names an exploited Outlook bug that may expose NTLMv2 hashes; no CVE ID or CVSS value was provided for it, so treat the patch volume as the actionable signal rather than any specific severity claim here.

Separately, an authentication bypass in Xen Orchestra tracked as VSA-2026-062 has no CVE identifier yet but is reported as exploited in the wild to gain admin access, with the vendor pointing to REST cookie parsing that collapses an authentication token into an empty object Hackers Exploit Critical Xen Orchestra Auth Bypass Flaw to Gain Admin Access. Operators of the virtualisation stack should assume exposure checks are time-sensitive.

Breaches

Nitori says an attacker reached a system on its network and may have stolen 37.62 million customer records — the "may" matters, and notification and identity-risk work will follow regardless of the final count Japanese furniture giant Nitori says hackers stole 37.6M customer records. Poland shut down its nationwide ePUAP platform, which handles document submission and communication with government agencies, after a cyberattack Poland shuts down government services platform ePUAP following cyberattack; the supplied material describes an availability impact on public-sector users and nothing about data loss. Maersk confirmed data was extracted from a local legacy system inherited through an earlier acquisition, as WhiteArmor demanded $5 million Maersk confirms data breach as WhiteArmor ransomware demands $5 million — inherited M&A estates remain a durable weak point, though no technical root cause was supplied.

Vulnerabilities

Zoom patched two flaws in Zoom Workplace for Windows, including CVE-2026-108186, an improper input validation bug rated 9.6 Critical that could let an unauthenticated user escalate privileges over the network Critical Zoom Workplace Flaw Could Allow Unauthenticated Remote Privilege Escalation. The supplied vector includes a user-interaction requirement, so this is not a silent remote takeover — the attacker needs a victim to do something. It is not in KEV, and no exploitation is reported; that is an absence of evidence, not proof of safety.

Research

Reporting traces the administrator of Qilin — described as the most active ransomware affiliate program, with at least 365 claimed victims in three months — to a 36-year-old man in St. Petersburg, based on a decade of operational security errors Who Runs the Ransomware Group Qilin?. Analysis: the identification rests on the reporting's own correlation of missteps, and the supplied excerpt does not detail charges or government confirmation, so treat it as attribution reporting rather than established fact.
