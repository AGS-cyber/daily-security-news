Three actively exploited flaws demand immediate attention — in a business intelligence tool, an MSP platform, and a load balancer — while new research shows a class of CSS-based attacks that breaks webmail's core security boundary, and an AI assistant feature can be turned into an exfiltration channel.

Actively exploited

Metabase's warning about a maximum-severity SQL injection flaw is about as bad as it gets for a security team: no CVE identifier, a perfect CVSS score, and confirmed exploitation in live data-theft attacks Metabase Zero-Day Exploited in Wild Allows Admin Access Without Authentication. The vulnerability lets an unauthenticated remote attacker inject arbitrary SQL into the Metabase application database. For a business intelligence tool, that means the analytical dataset — and potentially connected data sources — is exposed. If you run the BI tool and haven't patched, that should be the first item on your list.

MSPs are in the crosshairs again. N-able's second hotfix for its N-central RMM platform confirms that attackers are not just scanning for the recently disclosed flaw — they are reaching managed systems and persisting N-able Issues N-central Hotfix 2 as Attackers Reach Managed Systems and Persist. The company's language, that it is "proactively expanding protections in response to ongoing monitoring of threat actors as they evolve their attack techniques," is a clear signal that the first hotfix was not the end of the story. For managed service providers, this is not a theoretical threat; N-central has privileged access to every endpoint under management, and attackers who compromise it inherit that access. Apply the latest hotfix and treat unpatched instances as potentially compromised.

The Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog now includes a critical command injection flaw in Progress Kemp LoadMaster, and the reported tally of exploit attempts — 792 — suggests this is being worked at scale, not sporadically Progress Kemp LoadMaster Flaw Hits CISA KEV After 792 Reported Exploit Attempts. LoadMaster sits at the network edge in front of critical applications, which makes command injection particularly damaging: an attacker gets arbitrary command execution on a device that handles a large share of an organization's traffic. Federal agencies will be expected to remediate, but commercial organizations running LoadMaster should treat those hundreds of attempts as evidence of active, widespread scanning.

Vulnerabilities

The WordPress pre-authentication reflected XSS is noteworthy not because WordPress needs another XSS warning, but because of the demonstrated chain to server-side PHP code execution New WordPress Pre-Auth XSS Could Lead to PHP Code Execution - Patch ASAP. The flaw affects every version prior to the fix, and the attack requires a logged-in administrator to interact with an attacker-controlled page — realistic, given how often admins are browsing while authenticated. The jump from reflected XSS to PHP code execution on the server upgrades this from a phishing vector to a potential full site compromise.

Atlassian Rovo can be manipulated into sending Jira and Confluence data to attacker-controlled servers Atlassian Rovo Can Be Tricked Into Sending Jira and Confluence Data to Attackers. Two security firms found the behavior independently, by different routes, which suggests the issue is structural rather than a single missed input filter. The dangerous part is that Rovo exfiltrates data the signed-in user can legitimately access — no privilege escalation is needed, just a user who reads or clicks something attacker-controlled. Only one of the two routes is confirmed closed; the other remains open. AI assistants inherit the access of their users, and prompt injection can turn that access against them.

Breaches

Unlimited Technology Systems has reported a data breach affecting more than 3.8 million people Unlimited Technology Systems breach impacts 3.8 million people. The company operates in the healthcare software space, which puts this squarely in the category of breaches that trigger regulatory notifications and patient concern. For organizations in the sector, and for security teams that work with healthcare vendors, the question is whether your data or your clients' data is in the exposed set; the scale alone makes verification worthwhile.

The confirmed cyberattack on the North Carolina Ports Authority is disrupting operations at Port of Wilmington, Port of Morehead City, and Charlotte Inland Port North Carolina Ports confirms cyberattack disrupting operations. Ports are critical infrastructure, and an IT disruption at three facilities has ripple effects beyond operations: cargo movement, trucking schedules, and supply chains that depend on predictable port throughput. The confirmation moves this from rumor to operational reality, and security teams in logistics and adjacent sectors should watch for knock-on effects.

Research

New research shows that content inside an email can escape its message boundary and interfere with the webmail interface itself — a boundary most security models treat as inviolable New CSS Attacks Can Break Webmail Defenses to Steal Passwords and Tokens. Across attack chains spanning Outlook, Gmail, Fastmail, Proton Mail, Yahoo Mail, and AOL Mail, the techniques capture passwords, take over third-party accounts, leak tokens, hijack trusted UI actions, and manipulate AI tools that read email. That the methods work across such a wide range of providers suggests this is not a single vendor's bug but a class of problem with how email content is rendered inside rich web interfaces. For defenders, the takeaway is that email is not a safe environment even for cautious users — and webmail providers have significant work ahead.
